# Open customer decisions before live release

The detailed authoritative checklist is `docs/CUSTOMER_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md`.

- Confirm legal company name, provider/owner, ladungsfähige address, telephone, email, and WhatsApp use.
- Confirm tax status or VAT ID and any applicable professional title, chamber, or registration.
- Confirm the actual scope and priority of every advertised service.
- Confirm which electrical and sanitary tasks, if any, are permitted own work and which require coordination with qualified contractors.
- Confirm the real service area.
- Confirm the exact image rights and approved filenames for every published project group.
- Either approve an exact biography in writing or retain the neutral public business description.
- Approve the final design, copy, and hero image.
- Recheck the privacy notice against the final host and communication setup.
- Keep `previewMode` enabled, the custom domain unset, and the pull request in draft state until all applicable items are complete.

Explicitly excluded unless separately evidenced: project counts, experience years, fixed prices, guaranteed dates, response times, free site visits, warranties, team size, professional qualifications, broad electrical or sanitary installation claims, and brand or material claims.
